What the four contaminations actually are

Look-ahead

A column carrying information from the future, or a timestamp after the cutoff the research declared. The strategy knew something on Tuesday that nobody knew until Friday.

Same-bar execution

Deciding and filling at the same price. The most common way to manufacture a profitable backtest without noticing you did it.

Unbound results

The results declare a dataset hash that does not match the dataset supplied. The numbers were not produced from the data they claim.

An unaccounted search

Fifty-eight variants tried, one reported. The deflated Sharpe ratio prices that in, and a result that looked strong often survives it at roughly zero.

What you get

  • A content-addressed SHA-256 for every input the audit accepted.
  • A tri-state verdict — PASS, NO_GO, or INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE — with each finding labelled DEFECT (an error in the method) or RESULT (a correctly measured outcome, such as not beating your benchmark).
  • Check-level findings in deterministic JSON, plus a standalone HTML report.
  • A digest your own counterparty can recompute from the report's bytes, with no access to your data and no trust in me.
  • A thirty-minute walkthrough of the findings and a short remediation list.

The boundary

Your data does not leave your machine

The audit reads bounded local CSV or JSON. It imports no customer module, evaluates no expression, shells out to nothing, and opens no socket. Delivered reports can be redacted so they carry file names rather than paths.

I never take credentials

No API keys, no account exports, no order endpoints, no personal data. If your package contains any of it, that is a reason to stop, not to proceed.

Nothing here authorizes money

Every verdict permanently exports real_money_authorized=false and expected_profit_established=false. Constructing a verdict with either set to true raises an exception — it is an invariant of the code, not a promise in a document.

What this is not

  • Not a claim that your strategy will make money. The audit does not score expected returns and cannot.
  • Not an independent reproduction of your numbers. Your costs, benchmarks, net return and execution lag are read as declared, never recomputed from the data. The honest claim is that a package is internally consistent and byte-bound — which is a narrower thing than the word "audit" invites you to assume, and I would rather say so now than argue about it later.
  • Not statistical validation in full. Walk-forward independence, concentration and capacity are outside this scope rather than quietly represented as covered.
  • Not investment advice, signals, custody, pooled capital, or managed accounts.
